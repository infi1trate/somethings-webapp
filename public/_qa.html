Care record — QA contact sheet

Live app in iframes, same origin, at full size. Each frame is the real page — scroll inside it. Persona is set to supervisor for the acting states and switched per frame where noted.

Three things to drive by hand 1 — Press Approve with an empty provider note: the error appears at the field, focus moves there, and it is announced. 2 — Press a suggested-question pill in the rail: thinking dots, then a structured answer with real score chips and a link that scrolls History. 3 — Flip the persona toggle at the bottom of any frame to Mentor: everything stays readable, every action affordance disappears, nothing goes grey.
